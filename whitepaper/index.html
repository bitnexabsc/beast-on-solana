Beast on Solana ($BEASTSOL) is a community-driven memecoin inspired by the Beast character from Cortex Vortex. Built on Solana for its speed and low transaction costs, $BEASTSOL represents a return to the roots of memecoin culture: fair launches, community ownership, and transparent operations. There are no VCs, no presales, and no team allocations. Every holder starts on equal footing.

Name: Beast on Solana
Ticker: $BEASTSOL
Chain: Solana (SPL Token)
Total Supply: 1,000,000,000
Buy/Sell Tax: 0%
Team Allocation: 0%

Launched via Pump.fun's bonding curve model. Once the market cap threshold was reached, all liquidity was automatically migrated to Raydium and LP tokens were burned.

The dev wallet holds 100,000,000 tokens (purchased with 5 SOL at launch). Every $50K market cap milestone triggers a public burn of 2,500,000 tokens (2.5% each). At $2M market cap, the dev wallet reaches ZERO. All burns are verified on Solscan and posted publicly within 5 minutes of execution.

A fixed reward pool of 50,000,000 tokens is allocated for community tasks. Rewards are permanent and do not change with market conditions:

Verified X Profile: 20,000 + 50,000 bonus tokens
Non-Verified Profile: 20,000 + 50,000 bonus tokens
YouTube / Influencer: 1,000,000 tokens

All distributions are posted publicly with recipient wallet and Solscan TX hash.
