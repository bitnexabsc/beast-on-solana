CPANEL DEPLOY STEPS

1) Open cPanel File Manager.
2) Go to public_html (or your domain document root).
3) Upload cpanel-bootstrap-site.zip.
4) Extract it.
5) Move all extracted files/folders directly into public_html root.
   You should have index.html directly in public_html.
6) Visit your domain.

If homepage does not update:
- Clear browser cache.
- Clear cPanel cache/CDN cache (if enabled).
